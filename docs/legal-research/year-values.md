## Notes on the sources

- ČSSZ's table of minimum bases and its January 2026 overview show 235 044 Kč (19 587 Kč a month, 40 %) for 2026, from before the July 2026 change. The current act (§ 14 odst. 5), ČSSZ's July 2026 notice and Finanční správa's 9 162 Kč band 1 all use 35 %, so the tests use 205 668 Kč.
- Finanční správa's employee page labels 1 762 812 Kč as the 2025 threshold too; for 2025 it is 36 × 46 557 = 1 676 052 Kč. The tests assert only 2026.
- The 2024 year sheet's minimum SP base of 131 904 Kč (PRD known issue 10) equals the 2024 minimum for a *new* OSVČ, not the regular 158 292 Kč.
- The 2024 and mid-2026 rules are quoted from the official e-Sbírka wordings of those dates in [history.md](history.md).
- Tests that use these values: `osvc-regimes.test.ts`, `osvc-plan.test.ts`, `*.law.test.ts`.

### Mismatches and gaps

- **Donation cap, 2022 to 2026 (rule scope).** The statute's general cap is 15 % ("V úhrnu lze odečíst nejvýše 15 % ze základu daně"), and the app uses 15 %. Finanční správa's 2026 general page says: "Maximální limit 30 % ze základu daně platí pro roky 2022 až 2026." (in the paragraph on gifts connected with the invasion of Ukraine). The app does not model that 30 % limit. This is a missing rule, not a different number for the same rule.
- **§ 38lk odst. 7 is the tax part only.** The statute gives 4 963 Kč and 9 320 Kč as the income-tax advance for bands 2 and 3. The band totals of 16 745 and 27 139 Kč add the social and health advances, which come from the bases in § 14 odst. 3 of zákon 589/1992 Sb. and § 3a odst. 5 of zákon 592/1992 Sb.
- **Income tax (`incomeTax`).** Only the credit amounts were checked in this pass. The rates (15 %, 23 %), the mortgage caps (150 000 Kč, and 300 000 Kč before 2021), the donation rules and the child bonus minimum have not been checked against a second source, so `incomeTax` stays *researched* for 2024 to 2026. This is why the 2026 assertion in `src/data/tax-years.test.ts` (no unverified values) fails.
- **Employee rates for 2024 and 2025.** The health split (4,5 %) and the 2025 social rate have one source, so they stay *researched*.
- **Not checked here.** The other status-less keys (for example `socialMinimumShare`, `sicknessEligibilityThreshold`, `reductionThresholds`, `generalAssessmentBase`) were not part of this pass. The app treats a key without a status as verified, so they are still shown as verified.
